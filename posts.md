**Title**

I emailed 30+ prop firms the same 6 questions and made them answer in writing. Here's where the support desk and the rulebook disagree.

**Body**

I stopped reading pricing pages and started opening tickets. Same six questions to every firm:

1. Is the daily limit measured on floating equity or closed balance?
2. Does the drawdown floor trail, or is it static from the initial balance?
3. Are EAs allowed on the FUNDED account, not just the evaluation?
4. Is a VPS allowed?
5. Is there a consistency rule?
6. India accepted, and what is the total allocation cap?

Eleven answered clearly enough to act on. Things I did not expect:

**Static vs trailing is worth more than the percentage.** FundedNext Stellar Instant and CTI Instant both carry a 6% floor. FundedNext's trails your equity high, so every good day tightens the noose behind you. CTI's is static from the initial balance and never moves. Same number. Run both through the same random days and the static one survives far longer and pays out roughly twice as many times. If you check one thing, check whether the floor moves.

**BrightFunded can restrict your account after you have paid and passed.** In writing: "our Risk Department may determine that your funded account is subject to risk limitations such as: A reduced leverage factor of 5. A lot size limit. A daily limit restriction." Several funded holders report an undisclosed 1% daily cap. To their credit they said this plainly when asked directly, which is more than most firms managed. But a 1% daily cap makes the seat worthless for most intraday strategies.

**CTI's news rule contradicts itself inside one reply.** "News trading is allowed if you're trading manually, but it's not allowed in automated trading." Two paragraphs later: "if a trade was opened as part of your normal trading strategy and you happen to hold the position while a major news event occurs, this is not considered a violation." If you run an EA, get that settled before you pay.

**Allocation caps are real and they vary.** Equity Edge confirmed $200,000 total. FundedNext $300,000. FundingPips $400,000. If you plan to stack accounts at one firm, ask first.

**FundingPips bans VPS.** Home PC only. For a US-session strategy from Asia that means your machine is awake past midnight every weekday.

**The funded-stage EA trap.** Several firms permit EAs in the evaluation and ban them once funded. Never ask "are EAs allowed". Ask "are EAs allowed on the funded account".
